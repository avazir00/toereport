How to use

1. Dictate

  • Tap the big microphone and speak naturally. Say the section first: "Tricuspid valve. Quadricuspid tricuspid valve with the anterior leaflet consisting of two scallops...", then "Right ventricle. Dilated with reduced systolic function, FAC 29.2 percent."
  • Each section header also has its own microphone that dictates straight into that section.
  • Say measurements with their names: "AP diameter 53 millimetres", "3D vena contracta area 0.76", "EROA 0.5", "regurgitant volume 41 mils", "aorto-mitral angle 118 degrees", "E over E prime 10".
  • Tap Apply to report. The app fills the numbers, sets the dropdowns it recognises and places the tidied narrative in the right section. Check the log, then edit anything below.
  • If live recognition is unavailable (older iPhone, or the page is not served over https), tap in the box and use the keyboard microphone. Everything else works the same.

2. Smart paste

  • Paste the measurement table from the scanner or a spreadsheet (section, label, value per line) and tap Import.

3. Images

  • Add screenshots or photos of the echo screen (EROA, 3D VCA, aorto-mitral angle, 3D Auto MV, 3D TV analysis). Choose a caption and the linked section.
  • Crop out the patient banner before adding. Images are embedded in the Word document and attached when emailing from an iPhone.

4. Output

  • Preview shows the finished report with images; copy the text or print to PDF.
  • Email on iPhone opens the share sheet with the report text, the Word file and the images, so choose Mail. On desktop it opens your mail app with the text and downloads the Word file separately.
  • Word downloads a .docx with headings, bullets and the images.
  • Your draft is saved automatically on this device until you tap New.

iPhone tips

  • Open the page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen for a full-screen app.
  • Live dictation needs the page to be served over https (any static host, e.g. GitHub Pages or Netlify). Opening the file directly from Files uses the keyboard microphone instead.
